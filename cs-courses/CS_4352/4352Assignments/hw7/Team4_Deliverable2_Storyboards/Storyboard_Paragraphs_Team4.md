## Storyboard 1 – _Group Volunteering with Friends_

**Author of storyboard:** Stanley Gerzim
**Paragraph written by:** Jonathan Gonzalez

At home, Isaiah ponders how to spend his weekend.
He takes out his phone and clicks around to find ways to spend his time with his friends.
He finds a volunteering opportunity and creates a group to invite.
He invites a few friends and is excited to volunteer his time.
All of Isaiah’s friends accept his invitation to volunteer at the local Food Bank.
Isaiah and his friends are happy to be helping out in the community.
Isaiah’s friends erupt in happiness and are thankful for the invite.
The app Isaiah used to plan and organize the event with his friends clearly states how helpful they were to the cause via a congratulatory message.

## Storyboard 2 – _First-Time Volunteer Onboarding_

**Author of storyboard:** Joshua Gambrell
**Paragraph written by:** Jonathan Gonzalez

While scrolling on his phone, Daniel comes upon a cool park but initially hesitates to sign up.
Daniel is a bit shy and feels unprepared in how to best approach the volunteering event.
As he continues to get details of the event in the app, his worries quickly fade after coming across instructional videos for the local Food Bank.
Daniel's confidence increases after watching the short educational video on the app.
With his newfound confidence, Daniel signs up to volunteer.
Upon arriving, Daniel is welcomed with open arms and by the supportive staff at the local Food Bank.

## Storyboard 3 – _Finding a Values-Aligned Opportunity_

**Author of storyboard:** Jaden Flores
**Paragraph written by:** Jonathan Gonzalez

After watching an inspiring TV show, Lily wonders “Tom is a volunteer firefighter, but I can’t do that”.
Lily thinks there must be something out there that is more appropriate for her.
Lily searches for different opportunities online but struggles to find something that fits her values.
She then comes across an app that filters local volunteering opportunities to closely align with her values.
Through the app, she schedules and attends a local kitchen that serves food to people in need.
